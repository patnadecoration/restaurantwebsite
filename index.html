Our Heritage

Passion for Culinary Perfection

Founded in 2015, Spice & Sizzle was born from a deep love for rich aromatic spices, open charcoal woodfire grilling, and farm-fresh ingredients. We bring together ancient culinary heritage and contemporary gastronomy under one luxury ambient roof.

Every dish tells a story—from hand-pounded organic spices imported directly from coastal farms to our 72-hour slow-fermented pizza dough and prime cuts smoked over hickory wood.
